# CV / Resumé

**Resume Writing (Top 1%)**

- **Storytelling:** Tell a story rather than listing duties.
- **Quantification:** Use numbers to prove achievements.
- **Focus:** The resume should focus on the *reader* (employer), showing how you solve *their* problems.
