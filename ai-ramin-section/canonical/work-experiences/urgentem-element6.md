# Urgentem - Element6 Climate Risk Analytics

Ramin worked at Urgentem as Product Manager for a climate data platform from Oct 2019 to Nov 2020.

## Tradeoff Example

Internal roadmap prioritised Scope 3 data as a differentiator, but a key client needed Scope 1 data for a specific company.

Ramin initiated bi-weekly meetings with key users to align development with customer needs while managing internal priorities.

## Pricing Story

The interview draft describes a conflict over usage-based pricing versus tiered pricing.

Ramin argued for tiered pricing because unrestricted exploration inside a tier would produce better product usage data and encourage clients to discover more of the platform's value.

The team landed on a tiered model with a higher initial limit of 500 companies.

Use this as a behavioural story only after verification and rewriting.
