# Deity AI - Conversational AI Product

## Summary

Ramin worked at Deity AI as Product Manager for a chatbot-mediated dating/social product from Jul 2017 to Oct 2019.

This role is important because it is early evidence of conversational AI product thinking.

## Product Work

The product included:

- profiles with one-liner, one picture, favourite places, Deity personality, Deity icon, match rating, and circles
- Circles with intelligent match/group visualisation, group conversations, invitations, discover nearby, conversation ideas, and conversation time-out
- Deity features for self-understanding, experience planning, high-quality conversation, and connecting with places
- a chatbot conversation mediator
- Bluetooth matching concept for matching on the tube

The source says Ramin ideated and implemented an AI conversation mediator chatbot that improved chat retention by over 50 percent in its first 3 months of release. This metric should be reviewed before public chatbot use.

## Backlog Philosophy

The two core differentiators were:

- Bluetooth matching
- chatbot conversation mediator

Ramin used these to resist feature creep and keep 80 percent of the backlog focused on the two differentiators.

## Failure Story Candidate

The interview draft says that in early Deity AI work, Ramin was eager to please and said yes to too many senior stakeholder feature requests. This diluted roadmap focus and contributed to weak traction and failure to raise the next round.

The learning:

- evaluate requests against strategy
- prioritise user needs
- communicate assertively with senior stakeholders
- avoid feature creep

Use this only as a behavioural story after rewriting into a public-safe, non-blaming version.

## What This Proves

- Early conversational AI product experience.
- Social/dating product thinking.
- Backlog focus around differentiators.
- Learning from feature creep and stakeholder pressure.
- Ability to convert failure into a stronger product management principle.
