# GroupM - Media Carbon Calculator

## Summary

Ramin worked at GroupM as Product Manager from Jan 2022 to Dec 2022.

This is one of the strongest public-facing proof stories for Ramin's portfolio because it combines product management, sustainability, data methodology, stakeholder alignment, and market-scale product impact.

## Product Work

Ramin helped manage the product division of GroupM's Global Innovation Group and translated carbon emissions methodology into product specifications for a media Carbon Calculator.

Local sources say the product measured the environmental impact of more than GBP 1 billion of media investment in 2022 and became widely used in the media sector.

The source also says Ramin worked with EY consultants, overseas development teams, stakeholders, clients, MarTech experts, and wider corporate teams.

## Market Evolution

The PM Experience source describes the product's market evolution in four stages:

1. Measurement: reporting emissions was initially enough.
2. Comparison: UX shifted toward kgCO2 per viewer and calculation output reports.
3. Transparency: tooltips, educational wikis, explanations, and visible figures became important.
4. Programmatic ad-level CO2: partnership with Scope3 became more important.
