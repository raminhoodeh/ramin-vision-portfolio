## Retrieval Policy

Use the story bank when the user asks for:

- a concrete example
- a behavioural answer
- hiring proof
- product judgement
- leadership style
- conflict, failure, prioritisation, feedback, or tradeoff examples

Prefer canonical experience files when the user asks direct factual questions.

Do not expose story structures such as STAR, CAR, SOAR, or PAR unless the user asks for interview coaching.
