# Tradeoff - Urgentem Scope 3 vs Scope 1

## Use This Story For

- "Tell me about a product tradeoff."
- "How does Ramin balance roadmap strategy and client needs?"
- "How does Ramin handle B2B customer pressure?"

## Public-Safe Story

At Urgentem, the internal roadmap prioritised Scope 3 data because it was a strategic differentiator. But a key client needed Scope 1 data for a specific company. That created a real product tension: keep building toward the differentiator, or respond to a concrete customer need.

Ramin's response was not to treat the client request as a one-off interruption. He initiated regular user meetings to better understand the need and align development with real customer priorities while still managing the internal roadmap.

## SPAR Structure

### Situation

Urgentem was building climate risk analytics where Scope 3 data was an important differentiator.

### Problem

A key customer needed Scope 1 data for a specific use case, creating tension between strategic differentiation and immediate client value.

### Action

Ramin initiated bi-weekly meetings with key users to clarify needs and make the roadmap conversation more evidence-led.

### Result

The story shows how he manages roadmap tension: not by blindly following either internal strategy or client pressure, but by creating a tighter feedback loop.

## What It Proves

- B2B product judgement.
- Customer discovery under pressure.
- Roadmap discipline.
- Climate data product knowledge.
- Ability to convert tension into structured learning.

## Avoid

- Do not imply Scope 1 replaced the Scope 3 strategy.
- Do not overstate the final implementation outcome unless more context is added.
