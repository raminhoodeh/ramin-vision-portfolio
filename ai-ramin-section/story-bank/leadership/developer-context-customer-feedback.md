# Leadership - Giving Developers Customer Context

## Use This Story For

- "How does Ramin work with engineers?"
- "What is Ramin's collaboration style?"
- "How does Ramin motivate technical teams?"

## Public-Safe Story

Ramin's view is that engineers do better work when they can see why the work matters. Customer feedback, roadmap context, and the product's overall direction make implementation feel connected to value rather than just tickets.

The principle is simple: developers should not only receive requirements. They should understand the user problem, the roadmap, and how their work supports the wider product outcome.

## Principle Structure

### Belief

Implementation quality improves when engineers understand the customer and the product context.

### Behaviour

Bring customer feedback, roadmap logic, and the "why" behind the feature into engineering conversations.

### Impact

The team can make better technical decisions because they understand the purpose, not just the task.

## What It Proves

- Cross-functional empathy.
- Technical communication.
- Product leadership beyond ticket writing.
- Ability to connect strategy to implementation.

## Avoid

- Do not make this sound like engineers need to be persuaded to care.
- Keep it practical: context improves decisions.
