# Competing Priorities - GroupM Digital Media Module

## Use This Story For

- "How does Ramin handle competing priorities?"
- "Tell me about a time Ramin changed roadmap direction."

## Public-Safe Story

At GroupM, the team initially had a broader ambition for carbon measurement across media channels. After consulting clients and industry experts, Ramin identified a more immediate need around digital media, where environmental impact was growing quickly and client demand was clearer.

The roadmap shifted toward a digital media module. The key product move was not just prioritising what sounded strategically important, but listening for where the market had the most urgent need and where the product could deliver value faster.

## PAR Structure

### Problem

The team had broad carbon-calculation ambitions but needed to decide where to focus effort first.

### Action

Ramin used client and expert input to identify digital media as a higher-immediacy opportunity, then helped redirect roadmap focus toward that module.

### Result

The source draft says this allowed the team to deliver useful client value faster and supported GroupM's position in sustainable media investment. Public deployment should verify exact impact wording.
