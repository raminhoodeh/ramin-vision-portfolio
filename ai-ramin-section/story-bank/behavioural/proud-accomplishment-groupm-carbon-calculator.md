## Public-Safe Story

At GroupM, Ramin worked on a carbon calculator for media investment decisions. The problem was that media teams needed a reliable way to understand the carbon impact of different media choices, but the underlying methodology was complex and not naturally product-friendly.

Ramin helped translate carbon emissions methodology into product specifications, working across consultants, stakeholders, clients, and development teams. The product needed to do more than calculate an output. It needed to make the methodology understandable and credible enough for teams to use in real commercial decisions.

The local source says the calculator measured the environmental impact of more than GBP 1 billion of media investment in 2022 and supported GroupM's sustainability positioning. This metric should be reviewed before public deployment.

## CAR Structure

### Context

GroupM wanted to measure the environmental impact of media investments. The challenge was turning a complex carbon methodology into a product that client teams could understand and use.

### Action

Ramin helped translate methodology into product specifications, worked with EY consultants and development teams, and pushed the product toward transparency through outputs, tooltips, explanatory content, and more useful comparison metrics.

### Result

The product became a strong supporting example for sustainability product management. Local sources say it measured more than GBP 1 billion of media investment in 2022 and supported client bids and sustainability conversations.
