# Weakness - Too Entrepreneurial at Perkbox Vivup

## Use This Story For

- "What is Ramin's greatest weakness?"
- "How does Ramin respond to feedback?"
- "Can Ramin adapt from startup to corporate environments?"

## Public-Safe Story

After moving from the startup environment of WLP into Perkbox Vivup, Ramin received feedback that he could be too entrepreneurial in a larger-company context. He was used to owning problems end to end, but a larger company required clearer role boundaries, stakeholder management, and more deliberate alignment.

He treated the feedback as useful rather than defensive. The lesson was to keep the ownership mindset while becoming more precise about when to lead, when to align, and when to respect established team boundaries.

## CAR Structure

### Context

Ramin moved from a startup environment into a larger company with more defined roles and processes.

### Action

He worked on clarifying responsibilities, understanding other teams' operating styles, and focusing his energy on the core product management expectations of the role.

### Result

The feedback became a useful adjustment: keep the entrepreneurial energy, but express it through clearer collaboration and stronger role awareness.

## What It Proves

- Growth mindset.
- Ability to receive feedback.
- Corporate adaptation.
- Self-awareness.
- Collaboration maturity.
