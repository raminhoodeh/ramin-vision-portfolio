# Feedback - Vivup ABIT Model

## Use This Story For

- "How does Ramin give feedback?"
- "How does Ramin receive feedback?"
- "How does Ramin handle difficult team conversations?"

## Public-Safe Story

Ramin's approach to feedback is early, specific, and relationship-based. The source material says he learned the ABIT model through Vivup leadership development: Action, Behaviour, Impact, Turnaround.

The value of the model is that it keeps feedback concrete. Instead of criticising a person, it describes the action, acknowledges the behaviour, explains the impact, and offers a practical turnaround.

## CAR Structure

### Context

Feedback can become vague or personal if the structure is weak.

### Action

Ramin uses a specific model: Action, Behaviour, Impact, Turnaround. He pairs that with regular 1:1s and relationship-building so feedback is not only delivered during problems.

### Result

The feedback becomes easier to act on, less defensive, and more useful for team alignment.

## What It Proves

- Emotional intelligence.
- Practical leadership.
- Specific communication.
- Ability to handle constructive feedback without making it personal.
