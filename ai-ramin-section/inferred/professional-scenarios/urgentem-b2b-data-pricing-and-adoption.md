# Inferred Scenario - B2B Data Product Pricing And Adoption

## Boundary

This is an inferred product-management scenario based on Ramin's Urgentem pricing story and B2B climate-data product experience.

## Likely Product Pattern

For a B2B data product, Ramin would likely treat pricing as a product-learning mechanism, not just a commercial decision. A pricing model can either encourage exploration and workflow discovery or discourage users from seeing enough of the product's value.

## Product Questions This Helps Answer

- How would Ramin price a B2B data platform?
- How would he think about usage-based versus tiered pricing?
- How would he balance sales incentives with product learning?
- How would he diagnose underused data-product features?

## Likely Approach

- Identify the buyer, user, value metric, and adoption barrier separately.
- Avoid pricing that punishes exploration before value is understood.
- Package the product so users can discover workflow value inside clear boundaries.
- Use feature usage and qualitative feedback to understand where data products become habit-forming.
- Keep bespoke client needs from overwhelming the strategic roadmap.
