## Likely Product Pattern

For a checkout or benefits journey, Ramin would likely avoid starting with UI tweaks. He would first diagnose whether the drop-off comes from comprehension, eligibility anxiety, perceived risk, form complexity, pricing, trust, or unclear next steps.

## Product Questions This Helps Answer

## Likely Approach

- Map the funnel and identify the precise step where intent weakens.
- Pair analytics with interview evidence to understand why users hesitate.
- Separate usability friction from trust, eligibility, or value-confusion issues.
- Reduce uncertainty in the flow before adding new incentives.
- Protect user satisfaction when adding monetisation or supplier-margin logic.
