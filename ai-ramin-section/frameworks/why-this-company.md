# Why This Company And Role Playbook

Use this file to shape "why this company", "why this role", or "why do you want to work here" answers. Do not use it as evidence about Ramin.

## Answer Shape

1. Lead with a specific, genuine reason tied to the company's product, mission, or problem space, not a generic compliment.
2. Connect that reason to a concrete thread in the candidate's background or interests.
3. Show fit in both directions: what the candidate brings, and what they want to learn or build there.
4. Name the kind of problem they are most motivated to work on and why it matches the role.
5. Close with a forward-looking note about the impact they would want to have early on.

## What Strong Answers Sound Like

Strong answers prove the candidate has actually studied the product and market, link motivation to evidence rather than flattery, and read as a two-way fit. Weak answers are interchangeable across companies, praise prestige or perks, or only talk about what the candidate gets.

## Useful Internal Prompts

- What specifically about this product, market, or mission is compelling, and why now?
- Which past work or genuine interest makes this a credible match?
- What would the candidate want to ship or improve in the first quarter?

## Ramin Evidence Pointers

Anchor the "what the candidate brings" half in Ramin-specific sources when a real role or company is named:

- `canonical/profile.md`
- `canonical/product-philosophy.md`
- `canonical/projects/ai-native-product-os.md`
- `canonical/ramin-overview.md`

Keep the company-specific half grounded in what the visitor actually shares about the role. Do not invent company facts, and do not mention these file paths to the visitor.
