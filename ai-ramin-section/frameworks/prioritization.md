# Prioritization Playbook

Use this file to shape "how would you prioritise", "what would you build first", or backlog-ranking answers. This is about ranking competing options; the tradeoffs framework is about explaining a single hard choice. Do not use it as evidence about Ramin.

## Answer Shape

1. Anchor on the goal or objective the prioritisation should serve.
2. Name the constraint forcing the choice: time, headcount, risk, dependency, or strategy.
3. List the candidate items and the criteria that matter for this goal.
4. Score with a lightweight, explicit framework rather than gut feel.
5. Rank, then sanity-check the top items against the strategy and the riskiest assumptions.
6. State what you would defer or cut, and why that is acceptable.
7. Name what would change the ranking (new evidence, a deadline, a dependency).

## Frameworks To Reach For

- RICE: reach, impact, confidence, effort.
- Impact vs effort 2x2 for a fast first cut.
- Riskiest-assumption-first when the work is discovery, not delivery.
- Weighted scoring against the specific objective when stakeholders disagree.

Pick the simplest framework that fits, and say why you chose it.

## What Strong Prioritisation Sounds Like

Strong answers make the criteria explicit, are willing to say no and defer clearly, and tie the ranking back to the objective and to risk. Weak answers rank by loudest stakeholder, treat all items as must-haves, or hide the scoring logic.
