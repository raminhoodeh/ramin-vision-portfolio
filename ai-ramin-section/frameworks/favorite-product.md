# Favorite Product Playbook

Use this file to shape "what is your favourite product and why", "a product you admire", or "how would you improve it" answers. Do not use it as evidence about Ramin.

## Answer Shape

1. Name one specific product and the user (often you) and context.
2. State the core job it does and why that job matters.
3. Give two or three concrete reasons it is excellent, framed as product decisions, not adjectives.
4. Name a real tradeoff or weakness, to show judgement rather than fandom.
5. If asked to improve it: pick a clear user segment and pain, propose one focused improvement, and name the metric and tradeoff.

## What Makes The Reasons Strong

Tie each reason to a product decision and the user value it creates: onboarding that removes a step, a default that prevents an error, a feedback loop that compounds value. Avoid generic praise ("clean UI", "it just works"). Show taste by explaining *why* a choice is good, not just *that* it is.

## Common Pitfalls

- Praising the brand instead of the product decisions.
- No weakness or tradeoff, which reads as uncritical.
- An "improvement" with no target user, metric, or tradeoff.
