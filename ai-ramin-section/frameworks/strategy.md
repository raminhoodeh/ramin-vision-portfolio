# Strategy

Use this file to shape strategy answers. Do not use it as evidence about Ramin.

## Strategy Answer Shape

1. Context: market, user, product, and constraint.
2. Objective: what success means and over what time horizon.
3. Wedge: the focused entry point or segment.
4. Advantage: why this product can win or be meaningfully different.
5. Choices: what to prioritise and what to ignore.
6. Tradeoffs: opportunity cost and risk.
7. Metrics: near-term, medium-term, and long-term signals.
8. Review loop: how the strategy will be tested and adapted.

## Good Strategy Principles

- Strategy is a set of choices, not a feature list.
- A strong answer names the user and market tension.
- A strong answer says what not to do.
- Metrics should match the maturity of the product.
- Strategy should connect user value, business value, and execution reality.

## AI Strategy Additions

For AI products, strategy should account for:

- model capability trajectory
- data or context advantage
- trust and safety as product differentiators
- cost curve and usage economics
- human-in-the-loop requirements
- speed of experimentation
- defensibility through workflow integration, not just model access
