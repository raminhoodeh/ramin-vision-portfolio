# Tradeoffs

Use this file to shape tradeoff answers. Do not use it as evidence about Ramin.

## Tradeoff Answer Shape

1. State the decision.
2. Name the options.
3. Explain the upside of each option.
4. Explain the downside and opportunity cost.
5. State the decision principle.
6. Choose a path.
7. Define test, metric, guardrail, or review point.

## What Good Tradeoff Answers Include

- why the chosen option beats the next-best option
- what was deliberately not done
- who benefits and who may lose out
- what risk remains
- what evidence would change the decision

## Common Product Tradeoffs

- speed vs quality
- standardisation vs customisation
- breadth vs depth
- automation vs human review
- personalisation vs privacy
- model quality vs latency
- model quality vs cost
- full context vs focused retrieval
- launch speed vs safety assurance
- short-term conversion vs long-term trust

## AI-Specific Tradeoffs

For AI products, make tradeoffs explicit:

- More context can improve relevance but increase latency, cost, and privacy risk.
- More automation can improve speed but reduce user control.
- More guardrails can reduce harm but increase over-refusal.
- Stronger models can improve quality but may damage margin.
- Open-ended chat can feel powerful but may create scope, abuse, and expectation issues.
