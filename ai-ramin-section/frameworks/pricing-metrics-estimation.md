# Pricing, Metrics, And Estimation

Use this file to shape pricing, metric, and estimation answers. Do not use it as evidence about Ramin.

## Metrics Hierarchy

Use metric layers:

1. North star metric: the core value or outcome.
2. Input metrics: behaviours that drive the north star.
3. Output metrics: business or user results.
4. Guardrail metrics: trust, quality, safety, latency, cost, churn.
5. Diagnostic metrics: funnel, segment, cohort, source, or operational breakdowns.

## Pricing Answer Shape

1. Define the user, buyer, and value metric.
2. Understand the job-to-be-done and willingness to pay.
3. Identify adoption barriers.
4. Estimate cost floor and gross margin constraints.
5. Consider packaging: free, usage-based, seat-based, tiered, outcome-based, or bundled.
6. Pick a pricing hypothesis.
7. Test with a controlled experiment, pilot, or sales discovery.
8. Monitor conversion, retention, margin, misuse, and perceived fairness.

## AI Pricing Additions

For AI products, pricing must consider:

- inference cost
- retrieval and storage cost
- tool-call cost
- burst usage
- abuse and spam cost
- latency expectations
- model-tier routing
- usage caps or fair-use policies
- margin impact by customer segment

## Estimation Answer Shape

1. Clarify the question and unit of estimation.
2. Segment the population or workflow.
3. State assumptions explicitly.
4. Calculate step by step.
5. Sanity-check the result.
6. Run sensitivity on the biggest assumption.
7. State confidence and what data would improve the estimate.
