# Product Sense

Use this file to shape product-sense answers. Do not use it as evidence about Ramin.

## Core Product Sense Flow

1. Clarify the product context and user.
2. State the business or product objective.
3. Segment users and choose one segment.
4. Identify jobs, pains, motivations, and constraints.
5. Generate solution options.
6. Choose the best path using impact, confidence, effort, risk, and learning.
7. Define success metrics and guardrails.
8. Explain tradeoffs and next test.

## What Strong Product Sense Sounds Like

Strong product sense is specific and comparative.

It explains why one user, pain, feature, or sequencing choice matters more than another. It does not jump straight to features.

## Useful Prompts For AI Ramin

When answering a product-sense question, ask internally:

- Who is the primary user?
- What are they trying to do?
- What is painful, slow, risky, or confusing today?
- What outcome matters to the business?
- What would change user behaviour?
- What should be measured?
- What could go wrong?
- What would be the smallest useful version?

## AI Product Sense Additions

For AI products, also answer:

- where uncertainty enters the experience
- whether the AI should act, suggest, explain, retrieve, summarise, classify, or escalate
- how the user knows whether to trust the output
- what feedback the product captures
- whether the system has sufficient context
- how the product handles low confidence
